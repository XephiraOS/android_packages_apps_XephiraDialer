# Xephira Dialer v1.6.0 — White & Crimson Red iOS Liquid Glass Edition 🔮✨

This release implements the exact visual design system from the **Apple iOS Liquid Glassmorphism** mockups using **pure translucent white frosted glass** and **radiant Xephira Crimson red accents**.

---

### 🌟 What's New in v1.6.0:

* **🕊️ Luminous White Frosted Glass Canvas (`xephira_liquid_glass_window_bg.xml`)**:
  - Pure ethereal white frosted acrylic canvas (`#FFF8F9FA` to `#FFF1F3F6`) infused with soft radiant crimson ambient light bleeds along the margins.
  - Seamlessly unified across dialpad, recents, contacts, in-call, and settings.

* **🔮 3D Convex Crystal Glass Dialpad Keys (`pb_bg_dialpad_button_ripple.xml`, `dialpad_out_circle.xml`)**:
  - Keypad numbers (1–9, 0, *, #) redesigned as 3D convex white crystal glass lenses with sharp specular rims (`#FFFFFFFF`), subtle crimson reflection drops, and top specular shine arcs.
  - Number digits styled in crisp, elegant **Xephira Crimson Red** (`#FFE9012A`), exactly as specified in the iOS design mockup.
  - Illuminated radiant liquid crimson touch feedback on every keypress.

* **🔴 3D Convex Liquid Crimson Call Sphere (`pb_bg_single_sim.xml`, `pb_bg_dial_btn.xml`)**:
  - Large 3D convex liquid ruby crimson glass sphere with radiant drop glow, specular highlight arc, and pure white telephone handset.

* **🛸 Floating White Frosted Acrylic Dialpad Plate (`coui_panel_bg_without_shadow.xml`)**:
  - Translucent frosted white acrylic panel with 32dp rounded corners, a 1.5dp specular light rim, and an ambient crimson glow band along the top curve to completely prevent background text bleed-through.

* **📱 Pure Liquid Glass In-Call Experience (`xephira_liquid_glass_incall_bg.xml`)**:
  - In-call screens redesigned upon an ethereal white frosted glass backdrop with soft crimson ambient auroras blooming in the corners.
  - Floating white frosted acrylic caller card (`xephira_frosted_glass_card.xml`) with 28dp rounded corners, specular outline, and deep charcoal caller typography.
  - 3D convex crystal glass control lenses with radiant liquid crimson activation glow.

* **🛸 Floating White Frosted Glass Navigation Dock (`xephira_liquid_glass_nav_bg.xml`)**:
  - Floating frosted white glass capsule with 24dp rounded corners, specular light border, and radiant crimson active tab indicator.
